# PersianTextGuard — Persian entries.
#
# Opt-in: nothing uses these unless you pass a bundled list to ProfanityFilter. Treat them as
# a starting point for your community, not a finished list.
#
# ── How to read this file ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
#   [category]         every entry below it is of this WordCategory, until the next section
#   word or phrase     matched as whole words only (the default, and the safe one)
#   ~word              matched anywhere, inside longer words too
#   # ...              a comment
#
# Categories: profanity, sexual, insult, slur, harassment, mild. WordList.PersianDefault is
# everything except mild; WordList.All is everything; WordList.Bundled(...) picks categories.
#
# Every entry is normalised with PersianNormalizer on the way in, so Arabic yeh/kaf,
# zero-width characters and tatweel need no entries of their own. Neither do the evasions
# ProfanityFilter reads through: letters spaced or dotted apart («ک ی ر», «ج.نده»), a held key
# («کیییر»), digits and symbols for letters, filler inside a word («ک*یر»), masked letters
# («ک**ر») and a word split once («کی ر»). Persian suffixes are handled by the matcher too, so
# «جنده‌ها», «کیرتون», «کونیا» and «حرومزاده‌ای» need no entries. What IS listed here is spelling
# rather than typography: ص/ث for س, and the misspellings people settle on.
#
# ── What is deliberately NOT here ──────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# Public lists ban ordinary words, and a filter that rejects a normal message teaches people
# it is broken rather than that a word is banned. Left out on purpose, with the reason:
#
#   «کس»      also "person": «هر کس», «کس دیگه». Its insults are listed as compounds
#             and phrases instead («کسکش», «کس ننت», «کس خل»).
#   «کردن», «بکن», «دادن», «خوردن»   ordinary verbs.
#   «ساک»     a bag. «ساک زدن» and «ساک بزن» are listed.
#   «تخم»     an egg. «تخمی» and «تخم سگ» are listed.
#   «عن»      also «عن‌قریب».   «لخت»   also "bare".   «دول»   also "states" («دول اروپایی»).
#   «زنا», «صیغه», «بکارت», «واژن», «مقعد», «آلت تناسلی»   religious, legal and medical terms.
#   «داف», «ماچ», «دوست دختر»   not profanity.
#   «خر», «گاو», «سگ», «الاغ», «احمق», «خنگ»   everyday words before they are insults, and
#             «خر» in particular is ordinary in «خر پول», «خرکار», «خرحمالی».
#   «ترک», «لر», «عرب», «فارس», «رشتی»   ethnic NAMES, not slurs — banning them bans
#             the people. Slurs built on them belong in your own list as phrases.
#
# ── Sources ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# Curated by hand from, and credited in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md:
#   LDNOOBW (CC BY 4.0), LDNOOBW V2 (CC0 1.0), amirshnll/Persian-Swear-Words (Apache 2.0),
#   kaveh-dev/persian-bad-words (MIT)
# plus the ص/ث spellings and the inflections, which none of them carry.
